= Manage Argo Rollouts lifecycle and traffic
:context: manage-argo-rollouts-lifecycle-and-traffic

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
Manage Argo Rollouts lifecycle operations and configure traffic routing for progressive deployments. You can deploy, update, promote, and abort rollouts while controlling traffic distribution by using OpenShift Routes or OpenShift Service Mesh.

.Prerequisites
* You have logged in to the {OCP} cluster as an administrator.
* You have installed xref:../install/set-up-gitops-for-application-deployment.adoc#installing-openshift-gitops[{gitops-title}] on your {OCP} cluster.
* You have installed xref:../extend/understand-and-set-up-argo-rollouts.adoc#gitops-creating-rolloutmanager-custom-resource_understand-and-set-up-argo-rollouts[Argo Rollouts] on your {OCP} cluster.
* You have installed the xref:../extend/understand-and-set-up-argo-rollouts.adoc#gitops-installing-argo-rollouts-cli-on-linux_understand-and-set-up-argo-rollouts[Argo Rollouts CLI] on your system.
