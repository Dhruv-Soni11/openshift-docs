= Configure Argo Rollouts scope and plugins
:context: configure-argo-rollouts-scope-and-plugins

toc::[]

[role="_abstract"]
Configure Argo Rollouts scope, high availability, and traffic management plugins. You can set up cluster-scoped or namespace-scoped installations and enable traffic management and metric plugins for advanced deployment strategies.

.Prerequisites
* You have logged in to the {OCP} cluster as an administrator.
* You have installed xref:../install/set-up-gitops-for-application-deployment.adoc#installing-openshift-gitops[{gitops-title}] on your {OCP} cluster.
* You have installed xref:../extend/understand-and-set-up-argo-rollouts.adoc#gitops-creating-rolloutmanager-custom-resource_understand-and-set-up-argo-rollouts[Argo Rollouts] on your {OCP} cluster.
